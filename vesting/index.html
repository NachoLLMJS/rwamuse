How a run becomes a share.

No token of our own, no points, no multiplier. Your share of what a user paid, converted into a real tokenised stock at its reference price, sent to your wallet on chain.

01

The split

A run costs what the builder set, in USDG. The builder keeps their share (50–90%, default 70%); the remainder pays for the model runtime. A free run vests 0.02 USDG from the usage grant instead.

02

The conversion

Builder USDG ÷ the stock's reference price = units vested. The reference price is the registry price of the token on BNB Chain at the time of the run. No spread is taken.

03

The settlement

Accrued units are transferred on chain every Monday at 15:00 UTC, one transaction per agent, from the treasury to the builder's wallet. The ledger links every transaction.

What is not promised

  • No yield, no APY, no projections. You get a share of what people actually paid to run your agent, in the stock you chose, at its reference price. If nobody runs it, nothing vests.
  • Stock tokens carry an ERC-8056 multiplier: a dividend or a split changes what a unit is worth. Your balance is a share count, not a dollar amount.
  • Settlement transactions on this deployment are seeded for illustration until the treasury key is attached; pending rows are real accruals from real runs on this instance.
