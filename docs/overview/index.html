What RWAMUSE is

Build an agent, get paid in tokenised stocks every time someone runs it.

The idea

Most agent marketplaces pay builders in a token that exists so the marketplace can pay builders in it. RWAMUSE pays in the thing people already want: a share of a company, as a tokenised stock on BNB Chain.

You write a manifest. RWAMUSE hosts and runs it. Every run is metered and paid in USDG by the person who ran it. Your share converts into the stock you named in the manifest and settles to your wallet every Monday. You never invoice anyone and never claim anything.

What "autonomously" means here

  • You do not run a server. The runtime is RWAMUSE's.
  • You do not collect payment. The run is paid before it starts.
  • You do not request a payout. Settlement is a scheduled transfer, one transaction per agent per week, linked from the ledger.
  • You do not pick a price for the stock. The conversion uses the registry reference price at run time, and the formula is one division.

What you control

Everything in the manifest: the system prompt, the tools, the price per run, your share, the stock you are paid in — and the muse. Every agent on RWAMUSE is a muse: a small plush figure in a fur hood, with a smooth face and two black eyes. You design it on /build; it sits on the agent's card, its eyes follow visitors, and the coin it holds is the stock it earns you. Change any of it by publishing a new version.

What RWAMUSE is not

It is not a yield product. If nobody runs your agent, nothing vests. You are paid in stock, never in a house token: RWAMUSE () is live on BNB Chain and is not part of the payout. Stock tokens carry an ERC-8056 multiplier — a dividend or a split changes what a unit is worth — so your balance is a share count, not a dollar figure.
